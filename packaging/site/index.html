Mix and route your audio like Wave Link, on Linux.

LinuxLink splits your audio into channels, like Music, Games, Voice Chat and your microphone, and builds independent mixes from them: one for your headphones, another for your stream or recording, each with its own volume for every channel. Built on PipeWire.

Features

One mixer for every app, every mix and every mic.

LinuxLink builds its channels and mixes out of PipeWire, so every app and device you already use just shows up.

A routing matrix

Channels are rows and mixes are columns, and every cell has its own fader, mute and level meter. The faders work like Wave Link's, with ticks every 6 dB.

Apps stay in their channel

Put any installed app in a channel, by its real name and icon, Steam and Proton games included. LinuxLink keeps it there every time it starts.

Every mix is a virtual mic

Build up to five mixes and listen to any of them. Each one is also a microphone for OBS, Discord or a recorder, so your stream hears exactly what you choose.

Voice ducking

While you talk, your microphone lowers music and games in the mixes you choose. Pick the sensitivity, amount and speed, and boost quiet channels by up to 12 dB.

Devices that just work

Microphones and inputs become channels with real gain, mute and meters. Connect a Bluetooth headset and your mixes move to it, in stereo.

Stays out of the way

Close the window and LinuxLink keeps running in the tray, optionally from login. It remembers everything across reboots, has a local control API for Stream Deck style controllers, and updates itself.

Download

Get LinuxLink

Free, for any Linux distribution with PipeWire, on x86_64 and ARM64. Every file is also on the release page, with a SHA256SUMS file to check it.

Flatpak any distribution

One command adds LinuxLink's repository for your user and installs it. The runtime comes from Flathub, and Flatpak picks the right file for your computer.

flatpak install --user @BASE@/linuxlink.flatpakref

After that, flatpak update keeps it up to date, along with Discover and GNOME Software.

Add the repository by hand
flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists flathub https://dl.flathub.org/repo/flathub.flatpakrepo
flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists linuxlink @BASE@/linuxlink.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user linuxlink io.github.Okaza03.LinuxLink

Get started

From install to your first mix

LinuxLink runs on top of the sound system most distributions already use. Three steps get you mixing.

Check for PipeWire

LinuxLink needs PipeWire with pipewire-pulse, the default on Fedora, Ubuntu 22.10+, Debian 12+, Arch and most current distributions.

pactl info | grep "Server Name"
# Server Name: PulseAudio (on PipeWire …)
pactl, parecpulseaudio-utils, or libpulse on Arch. The packages install them.
Tray iconKDE, Cinnamon, XFCE; on GNOME, the AppIndicator extension

Put your apps in channels

Start LinuxLink from your app menu and click a channel, like Music. Tick or search for the apps that belong in it, and they'll land there every time they start.

Build your mixes

In My Mix, set how loud each channel is in your headphones, and pick them in the top-right corner. For streaming or calls, use the Stream mix and select LinuxLink Stream as the microphone in OBS or Discord.

The user guide explains everything, and setups has step-by-step recipes for OBS, Discord, recording and Bluetooth headsets.
